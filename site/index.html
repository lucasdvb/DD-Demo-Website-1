Self-care, shaped by the tide

Reservations
20°00′S · 57°35′E — Grand Baie, Mauritius Scroll

Marée is Grand Baie’s house of island self-care — a quiet sanctuary set between the lagoon and the sugarcane. Our rituals draw on what Mauritius has always known: warm basalt from the volcanic south, Bourbon vanilla, ylang-ylang and sugarcane from the fields, the mineral pull of the Indian Ocean. Each one is paired with modern recovery — a contrast circuit, infrared heat, lymphatic and breathwork — and hosted with the unhurried warmth of the island. Not a gym, not a beach club: a place to slow the pulse, alone or together, in time with the tide.

Our rituals

Every ritual at Marée follows the tide: a slow rise of heat, a pause at the turn, a long cool return. Each sequence is composed by our therapists with island ingredients and evidence-led recovery, so you leave lighter, steadier and more rested than when you arrived.
